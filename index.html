Hello, I'm Samer.

Your speaking companion. I turn a written speech into a calm, easy-to-read teleprompter, on a laptop, tablet or phone.

My name is Arabic, سامر, and means a companion in evening conversation: someone who keeps good company with words. That's what I try to be when you stand at the lectern.

What I do

  • Read your speech from a Word or PDF file, in English or Arabic.
  • Lay it out for speaking: title, salutations, sections, closing line, tight spacing for short lines and highlighted facts and figures.
  • Suggest a key message for every section, as a private cue for you.
  • Scroll at your pace in words per minute, and keep track of your time slot.
  • Travel with you: present here, export one file, or send the speech to your phone with a QR code.

Privacy

I run entirely in your browser. No account, no server, no tracking. Your documents are read on your device and your speeches are saved only in this browser. When you send a speech to your phone, it travels inside the link itself and is not stored anywhere.

Read me

1 · Getting started

  1. Drop a .docx or .pdf on the start page, or try the sample speech.
  2. Check the text in the editor. The live preview shows exactly what the teleprompter will show.
  3. Press ▶ Present, Export a file, or Send to phone.

2 · Writing in the editor

One lineOne paragraph (empty lines are ignored)
# ClosingStarts a section called “Closing”
**Opening remarks** at the topTitle
**key words**Emphasis (bold, underlined)
// or [pause]Pause mark ‖
> …Key message: a cue for you, not read aloud and not timed

Automatic: salutations (“Excellencies,” “Ladies and gentlemen,”) in yellow, numbers, percentages and amounts highlighted, short lines kept close together, “Thank you” as the closing line. Section and slot timing are shown above the text.

Use ⚙ for text direction (Arabic is detected automatically) and your time slot.

4 · Send to phone

Scan the QR code or open the link on your phone: the speech opens ready to present and is saved there too. QR codes hold about 800 words; longer speeches use Copy link or Share. Anyone with the link can read the speech.

5 · Files I understand

  • Word .docx (also from Google Docs, Pages or LibreOffice “Save as Word”). Old .doc files must be saved as .docx first.
  • PDF with selectable text. Scanned PDFs have no text to read; Arabic PDFs often come out scrambled, so use Word for Arabic.
  • Heading styles become sections; bold opening lines become the title; bold words become emphasis.
  • Images, comments, headers, footers, page numbers and footnotes are ignored. Accept tracked changes before importing.

6 · Working offline

Online version (offline mode): after your first visit with internet, I keep a copy of myself on your device, including the Word and PDF readers, the QR code maker and the fonts. From then on everything works without internet: opening Samer, importing, editing, presenting, exporting and Send to phone with QR code. When you are online again, updates load automatically.

Install me for one-tap access, like an app: use Install app at the top of the start page (Chrome, Edge, Android), or in Safari on iPhone/iPad tap Share → Add to Home Screen. A red ● Offline badge at the top tells you when there is no connection.

Online version, after one visitEverything works offline
Local file (index.html on your computer)Opens, edits, presents and exports offline. Importing Word/PDF and QR codes need internet (once per session)
Exported teleprompter filesAlways work offline, with the device's own fonts
Send to phone linkOpens offline on a phone that has visited Samer before; a phone that never has needs internet once

Good to know: the offline copy belongs to each browser on each device; private windows and clearing browser data remove it (and your saved speeches). Before an event without Wi-Fi, open Samer once on the device you will use, while you are still online.

7 · Technical caveats

  • Storage: speeches live in this browser only. They are not synced between devices, private windows forget them, and clearing browser data deletes them. Export or Send to phone to keep a copy.
  • Internet: see “Working offline” above. The very first visit always needs a connection.
  • iPhone / iPad: exported .html files opened from Mail or Files only show a static preview. Use Send to phone instead. Safari has no fullscreen: use Share → Add to Home Screen.
  • Screen sleep: I keep the screen awake on recent browsers (iOS 16.4+, current Chrome/Edge). On older devices set Auto-Lock to “Never”.
  • Automatic layout is a best guess. Always check sections, salutations and key messages before going on stage. Key messages are picked from your sentences, not written by AI.
  • Timing assumes a steady pace at your chosen words per minute; the setting is shared by all speeches.
  • Browsers: current Chrome, Edge, Safari and Firefox.
