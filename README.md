# Samer · your speaking companion

**Samer** turns a written speech into a calm, easy-to-read teleprompter on a laptop, tablet or phone.

**Open the app:** https://iah87-crypto.github.io/samer/

- Upload a Word (.docx) or PDF speech, in English or Arabic
- Sections, salutations, the closing line, facts and figures, and a key message per section are set up for you
- Edit the text like a document with a live teleprompter preview
- Present with speed in words per minute, a time-slot tracker, a reading band, presentation-clicker support and mirror mode
- Export one file, or send the speech to your phone with a QR code
- Works offline after the first visit, and can be installed like an app

Everything runs in your browser. No account, no server, no tracking: documents are read on your device and speeches are stored only in your browser.

Instructions and technical notes are in the app under **Read me**.
